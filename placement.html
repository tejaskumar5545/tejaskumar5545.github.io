Placement Roadmap

Follow this step-by-step plan to maximize your chances of getting placed.

1

Build Foundations (Month 1-2)

Strengthen your core CS fundamentals and programming skills.

  • Master one programming language thoroughly
  • Complete DSA basics: arrays, strings, linked lists
  • Revise OS, DBMS and CN fundamentals
  • Start solving 2 problems daily on LeetCode
2

Deep Dive (Month 3-4)

Advance your problem-solving and start company-specific prep.

  • Complete trees, graphs and dynamic programming
  • Solve 100+ LeetCode problems minimum
  • Practice aptitude and reasoning daily
  • Build 2-3 strong projects for your resume
3

Interview Ready (Month 5-6)

Polish your resume, practice mock interviews and apply.

  • Create an ATS-optimized resume
  • Practice mock interviews with peers
  • Prepare HR answers and STAR stories
  • Apply to target companies and take mock tests

Interview Preparation

Be ready for every round of the interview process.

👤
HR Interview Tips
  • Tell me about yourself — craft a 2-minute pitch
  • Why should we hire you? — highlight unique skills
  • Where do you see yourself in 5 years?
  • Strengths and weaknesses — be honest and real
  • Why this company? — research their products and culture
  • Salary expectations — research market standards
💻
Technical Interview Prep
  • Practice live coding on whiteboard or shared editor
  • Explain your thought process while solving
  • Know time and space complexity of every solution
  • Be ready for follow-up optimization questions
  • Revise OOPs concepts with real-world examples
  • Practice system design for senior roles
📈
Guesstimate Framework
  • Clarify the question before jumping to numbers
  • Break down the problem into smaller parts
  • Use top-down or bottom-up approach
  • State your assumptions clearly
  • Practice: coffee shops in Delhi, WhatsApp messages/day
  • Arrive at a logical final number with reasoning
📝
Resume Power Tips
  • Use action verbs: Developed, Implemented, Optimized
  • Quantify achievements: "Reduced load time by 40%"
  • One page resume for freshers — no exceptions
  • Tailor your resume for each company/role
  • Include GitHub, LinkedIn and portfolio links
  • Proofread twice — zero grammatical errors
