Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you register on EngiHub, we collect:

  • Full name, email address, and mobile number.
  • College name, branch, and semester.
  • Password (stored securely using industry-standard hashing).

We may also collect optional profile information you choose to provide (profile photo, date of birth, state, city, student ID).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and improve the EngiHub platform.
  • Authenticate your account and prevent fraud.
  • Send important account-related notifications.
  • Personalize your experience.

3. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • Secure password hashing (never stored as plain text).
  • Encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • Session management and CSRF protection.
  • Regular security reviews.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share anonymized, aggregated data for analytics purposes.

5. Cookies

EngiHub uses essential cookies for authentication and session management. We do not use tracking cookies for advertising.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your account and data.
  • Opt out of non-essential communications.

7. Children's Privacy

EngiHub is intended for engineering students aged 16 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 16.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

9. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us via our website or WhatsApp.
