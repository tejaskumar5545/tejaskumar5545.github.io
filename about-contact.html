About Us

What is EngiHub?

EngiHub is a student-friendly educational platform designed to help Engineering and Polytechnic students access important learning resources in one place.

We understand the struggle of finding the right notes, previous year papers, and practical files across scattered sources. EngiHub brings everything together in a clean, organized, and easy-to-use platform.

Whether you need semester-wise notes, lab manuals, coding practice, project ideas, or placement preparation material - EngiHub has it all.
